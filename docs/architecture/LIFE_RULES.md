# LIFE OS — LIFE RULES

**Status:** Authoritative Behavioral Principles  
**Last Synchronized:** September 2026 (Phase 1 Baseline)

---

## Rule 1 — Consistency Beats Intensity
Small repeated actions across months and years compound far more than occasional bursts of high motivation.
* **System Implication:** Emphasize streaks, consistency percentages, and rolling momentum over single-day heroics. The 14-day momentum history and weekly system score reflect this principle.

---

## Rule 2 — Systems Beat Motivation
Motivation fluctuates and is unreliable. A well-designed behavioral system makes the right action lower friction than the wrong one.
* **System Implication:** Life OS prioritizes structure (habits, weekly plans, focus timers, reviews) over gamified hype. The Brain Engine's directive exists to remove cognitive decision fatigue.

---

## Rule 3 — Track Reality, Not Intention
Many productivity tools record what users intended or hoped to do. Life OS records what actually happened.
* **System Implication:** Habit logs record real completions. Time logs record actual elapsed focus sessions. The events pipeline records immutable database mutations.

---

## Rule 4 — Reduce Friction
Any system that takes too long to operate will eventually be abandoned.
* **System Implication:** Every interaction follows: **Open → Log → Continue Life**. Minimal modals, keyboard shortcuts, and one-tap timers preserve this invariant.

---

## Rule 5 — Insight Over Data Overload
Accumulating data is meaningless without actionable synthesis.
* **System Implication:** Data Lab surfaces longitudinal trends and drift rather than raw tables. The Brain Engine condenses multi-domain metrics into a single high-priority directive.

---

## Rule 6 — Simplicity Wins
The user interface must remain calm, true-black, and focused even as backend analytics grow complex.
* **System Implication:** Clean typography, no distracting animations, and clear cognitive boundaries between reflection and execution.

---

## Rule 7 — Long-Term Multi-Year Record
Life OS is architected to preserve multi-year longitudinal personal data safely.
* **System Implication:** Schema design, foreign key constraints, RLS security, and indexed SQL views are optimized for years of continuous historical data.

---

## Rule 8 — Growth Is Multi-Dimensional
Human life spans multiple interconnected dimensions:
- **Discipline & Habits:** Mind OS
- **Execution & Objectives:** Productivity Hub
- **Skill Acquisition:** Learning OS
- **Physical Training:** Fitness OS
- **Focus & Deep Work:** Time OS
- **Financial Awareness:** Finance OS
- **Cognitive Reflection:** Mind OS Journal
