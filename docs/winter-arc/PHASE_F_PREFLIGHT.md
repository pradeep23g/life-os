# WINTER ARC — PHASE F DESIGN PRE-FLIGHT
**DATE**: 2026-09-09
**STATUS**: READY FOR SPECIALIST PROPOSALS

This document defines the architectural and visual constraints for the Phase F implementation, covering Progression, Data Lab, and Reports. 

---

## 1. PROGRESSION (Identity & Narrative)

### 1. Human Question
"Who am I becoming, and what chapters of my life have I lived to get here?"

### 2. Visual Dominance
- The passage of time (chapters, seasons, arcs).
- Earned thresholds and narrative identity shifts.
- A strong typographic, editorial feel representing personal history rather than a video game HUD.

### 3. Hidden / Progressive Information
- Raw numerical XP points, micro-leveling details, and mathematical breakdowns should be completely absent from the top layer.
- Mathematical gamification should only exist implicitly, visible only if the user explicitly drills down into the raw Brain Engine logs.

### 4. Unique Composition
- It is a **Personal History Book**, not an RPG character sheet. 
- Composition uses macro-whitespace, serif typography (Newsreader for editorial surfaces), and timeline-driven scroll architecture instead of dense cards and progress bars.

### 5. Existing Data Sources
- `useMissionControlSnapshot.ts` (Brain Engine `LifeState` and Momentum).
- `useEventsAnalytics.ts` (Historical consistency and streaks).
- System Events / Event Bus (identifying major threshold events and chapter shifts).

### 6. Fabricated Metrics Risk
- Inventing a "level" (e.g., Lvl 42) that doesn't actually exist in the underlying XP/momentum schema. 
- Fabricating fake "badges" or "achievements" that are hardcoded rather than derived from real historical milestones in the database.

### 7. Interaction Model
- Narrative scroll. As the user moves down the page, they scroll back through their previous chapters and milestones. 
- Read-heavy, quiet, non-interactive beyond vertical exploration. No "claim reward" buttons.

---

## 2. DATA LAB (Exploratory Observatory)

### 1. Human Question
"What hidden behavioral rhythms and correlations can I discover about my lived experience?"

### 2. Visual Dominance
- Temporal relationships and cross-domain overlap.
- Quiet, clinical instrument typography (Geist font).
- Anomalies and patterns rather than sheer volume.

### 3. Hidden / Progressive Information
- Standard generic KPIs (Total Tasks, Total Minutes, Total Workouts) should be suppressed. 
- Filter panels and complex query builders should be hidden behind progressive disclosure (or eliminated entirely in favor of smart default patterns).

### 4. Unique Composition
- An **Observatory Screen**, not Tableau.
- High-density data presented in a clinical, monochrome, terminal-like or spatial layout. Avoid brightly colored pie charts, basic bar graphs, and boxed widgets.

### 5. Existing Data Sources
- `useDataLabStore.ts` and associated data lab hooks.
- `useTimeAnalytics.ts` (Time OS distribution).
- `useEventsAnalytics.ts` (Cross-domain consistency).
- `useHabitWorkspace.ts` (Habit completion grids).

### 6. Fabricated Metrics Risk
- Plotting placeholder graphs or using `Math.random()` to fill out heatmaps. 
- Presenting "Insights" that are hardcoded strings rather than generated by the AI Brain Engine based on actual data intersections.

### 7. Interaction Model
- Panning and zooming across a timeline, or toggling layers of behavior (e.g., overlaying "Sleep" onto "Deep Work" to visually see the correlation). 
- Hover interactions for clinical precision tooltips.

---

## 3. REPORTS (Editorial Synthesis)

### 1. Human Question
"What actually happened during this period, and what does it mean for my trajectory?"

### 2. Visual Dominance
- Written synthesis and narrative formatting.
- Beautifully typeset pull quotes and summaries.
- A "printed document" aesthetic over a digital dashboard aesthetic.

### 3. Hidden / Progressive Information
- The raw data rows that make up the report are hidden. 
- Only the synthesized conclusion and the two or three most critical supporting data points are shown.

### 4. Unique Composition
- A **Field Report / Weekly Sunday Paper**. 
- Employs strict "Composition over Cards" and "Typography over Containers". The background should be clean, leveraging large typographic contrast (Newsreader serif) to guide the eye through the narrative.

### 5. Existing Data Sources
- `usePlanning.ts` (Weekly Review data, Weekly Plan Items).
- `useJournalEntries.ts` (Reflective synthesis).
- `useMissionControlSnapshot.ts` (Brain Engine momentum context).

### 6. Fabricated Metrics Risk
- Hardcoding "You had a great week!" instead of calculating the actual trend. 
- Faking AI text summaries. The report must rely on the actual user-entered reflections and tracked completion rates.

### 7. Interaction Model
- Purely read-only scroll.
- Potentially paging through previous weeks/months like flipping through a bound ledger. No configuration panels or date pickers on the surface; use subtle typographic navigation.
